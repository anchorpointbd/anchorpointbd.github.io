Transparent calculator 01

Electrical power.

P = k × V × I × PF

Theory: real power equals apparent electrical power multiplied by power factor. For a balanced three-phase system, k = √3.

Result will appear here.

Assumptions: sinusoidal steady state; balanced phases; RMS values. Not a cable, protection, or equipment sizing method.
