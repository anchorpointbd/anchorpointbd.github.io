01

What we may collect

We may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, organization, professional role, location, contact details, enquiry, preferences and any material you deliberately submit. Our services may also create limited technical records needed to operate, secure and understand use of the service, such as timestamps, requested pages, device or browser information and security events.

Do not submit passwords, recovery codes, restricted records, confidential employer or client information, or personal information about another person unless an approved process specifically requires it.

02

Why we use information

We use information to provide requested features, respond to enquiries, remember choices you ask us to save, improve usefulness, protect the service, prevent abuse, maintain necessary business records and meet applicable obligations. If we introduce an optional professional profile or research programme, its purpose and choices will be explained at the point of collection.

We do not intentionally sell personal information or use the service for behavioural advertising.

03

Website contact enquiries

The callback form collects your name, email address, optional organization, role and telephone number, preferred contact route, and the non-confidential message you choose to provide. Formspree processes the submission and makes it available in its service while also delivering a copy to info@anchorpoint.com.bd.

Anchor Point is responsible for access to and deletion of its Formspree dashboard and mailbox copies. Routine enquiries that do not become an engagement are deleted within 90 days after the final response. If an enquiry becomes contractual work, limited correspondence may be retained with the relevant business record, dispute or legal requirement. Do not use the form for drawings, credentials, controlled files, trade secrets or sensitive project information.

04

Interactive features

Some optional features process the text or values you enter to return a calculation, explanation, search route or other result. A short notice beside the feature explains when information leaves your device. Do not enter confidential, project-identifying or sensitive personal information into a public interactive feature.

Calculations and general explanations are informational. They do not create a professional appointment or transfer responsibility for consequential decisions.

05

When information may be shared

We may use service providers for communication, hosting, security, storage, analytics and interactive functionality. They receive only the information reasonably necessary for their role and process it under their own legal and contractual responsibilities. We may also disclose information where law requires it, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a properly governed organizational transaction.

06

Retention and deletion

We aim to keep personal information only as long as needed for its stated purpose, security, contractual records, dispute handling, backup integrity or applicable law. Different records require different periods. Backup copies may take additional time to expire. A feature designed as local-only will state that clearly and will not intentionally transmit the entered content.

07

Security and sensible limits

We apply access controls, separation of public and private functions, protected credentials and proportionate operational safeguards. No internet service can promise absolute security. Contact us before sending sensitive material so that purpose, access, retention and the appropriate channel can be agreed.

08

Your choices and requests

Contact info@anchorpoint.com.bd to ask what information we control about you or to request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent or another available privacy choice. We may need to verify identity and may retain limited records where security, contracts, disputes or law require it.

This notice will be revised when our legal identity, obligations or data practices materially change. Material changes receive a new version and effective date.
