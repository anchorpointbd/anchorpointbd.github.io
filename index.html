The Anchor Point approach

Projects fail in the gaps between a requirement and its delivery.

We make those gaps visible before they become costly: missing dependencies, unclear scope, difficult reports, misaligned specifications, and the absence of the right specialist at the right time.

Understand

We clarify the operating context, constraints, information already available, and the outcome you need.

Diagnose

We identify gaps, risks, dependencies, and questions that need an answer before a decision is made.

Present options

We turn findings into practical choices, with scope, priorities, and next actions made clear.

Coordinate

Where appropriate, we help connect and coordinate the expertise required for the next stage.
