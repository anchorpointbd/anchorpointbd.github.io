A small demonstration

The first question is not always the real one.

This is a constructed example of the reasoning—not a client project or design recommendation.

WHAT YOU MIGHT ASK
“The supplier says the larger pump is safer. Should we accept it?”
Bigger pump?→What must the system move, and against what pressure?→Right-sized decision

Before choosing a pump, we make the real operating need visible:

  • How much liquid must move, and how quickly?
  • What pressure or resistance must it overcome?
  • How do energy use, wear and running cost change?
  • What failure or interruption are we preventing?

THE CLEARER QUESTIONWhat does the system actually need—and which option meets it without unnecessary cost, wear or risk?
