About Anchor Point

Your Trusted Local Partner in Bangladesh

Anchor Point is a registered consultancy firm based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, established in December 2025. We specialize in technical commissioning, project coordination, and consultancy services for international manufacturers and suppliers operating in the Bangladesh market.

Our team brings deep expertise in marine and industrial systems, combined with strong local networks across Chattogram port, naval establishments, and commercial sectors. We bridge the gap between foreign principals and local execution — reliably, professionally, and accountably.

Anchor Point operates as an authorized local service representative for international equipment suppliers, handling end-to-end project delivery including site coordination, commissioning supervision, documentation, and after-sales warranty support.
