04 / AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

Clear thinking does not borrow authority.

Anchor Point can frame, analyse, compare and coordinate. Where work requires regulated, licensed or specialist authority, that responsibility stays with the properly appointed professional. The client retains the final organizational decision.

05 / THE INSTITUTION

Built in public,
without pretending
to be finished.

Anchor Point is an early-stage engineering reasoning institution. Its ambition is larger than a consultancy menu: useful tools, public resources, connected specialist capability and a durable practice of clearer decisions.

That ecosystem has to be earned. We will describe what exists, identify what is still being built, and avoid implying people, projects or authority we cannot verify.
