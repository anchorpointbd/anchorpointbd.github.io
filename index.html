Our philosophy

We do not begin
with an answer.

Anchor Point exists to prevent the first convenient solution from hiding the real difficulty.

A solution chosen too early can make the real problem more expensive.

Our permanent principles

What will remain true as we grow.

Ⅰ

Tell the truth—even when it is inconvenient.

An uncomfortable constraint is more useful than a polished assumption that fails later.

Ⅱ

Compare options before creating lock-in.

You should see the benefits, consequences and realistic room to change before committing.

Ⅲ

Connect the whole system.

Technical, commercial, legal, financial and human interfaces often decide whether the work succeeds.

Ⅳ

Keep the reason—not only the result.

Future teams should understand what changed, why it changed and when the decision should be reviewed.
