01

General information

Public content is provided for general information, orientation and education. It may be incomplete, simplified, out of date or inapplicable to a particular jurisdiction, asset, organization or operating condition.

02

Engineering calculators

Calculators show formulas and stated assumptions to support understanding. Their outputs are estimates—not designs, certifications, procurement instructions, protection settings, safe working limits, welding procedures, inspection acceptance, operating instructions or evidence of compliance.

Before consequential use, verify inputs, units, equation applicability, material and environmental conditions, applicable editions of codes, uncertainty and independent competent review.

03

AI theory assistant · experimental beta

The assistant may explain theory and suggest verification questions. It may be unavailable, inaccurate or inconsistent because it depends on free third-party model capacity. It must not approve designs, select safety-critical equipment, prescribe site work, interpret a code as final authority, make an emergency decision or replace a competent professional.

Do not submit client names, project measurements, drawings, credentials, personal data, commercial material or confidential information. Use constitutes confirmation that the prompt is generic and public.

04

No professional appointment through browsing

Using the website, sending an enquiry or receiving an initial response does not create an engineering appointment, fiduciary duty, agency, confidentiality obligation, partnership, employment relationship or guarantee of service. Any professional work requires a separate written scope defining parties, people, authority, exclusions, information handling, deliverables, fees, review and acceptance.

05

External sources and links

Links are offered to help visitors approach original or legitimate sources. Anchor Point does not control third-party availability, security, licensing, revision status or accuracy. Verify publisher, edition, jurisdiction and applicability.

06

Acceptable use

Do not attempt unauthorized Vault access, evade rate limits, probe credentials, disrupt services, upload malicious content, misrepresent outputs as certification, or use educational material to bypass competent safety review.

07

Your responsibility

You remain responsible for decisions made using public material. Stop and obtain competent, authorized review whenever a question affects life safety, structural integrity, electrical protection, pressure containment, navigation, environment, public funds, legal compliance or other consequential outcomes.

Questions about these terms may be sent to info@anchorpoint.com.bd. These public terms may be revised before production launch when verified legal identity and jurisdictional review are completed.
