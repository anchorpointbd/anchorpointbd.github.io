# Public proof layer

## Purpose

The proof layer closes the gap between Anchor Point's institutional ambition and the evidence a serious visitor needs before engagement. It must never solve that gap by inventing clients, projects, credentials, partnerships, registrations, certifications, government authority, or outcomes.

## Public information architecture

- `index.html#proof`: proof gateway on the homepage.
- `evidence.html`: current evidence status, intended fields, constructed demonstration cases, output structures, capability boundaries, and the engagement path.
- `trust.html`: data classification, channel rules, provider map, AI boundary, Vault safeguards, and missing institutional verification.
- `privacy.html`: operational versioned privacy notice for the current launch-candidate stack.
- `terms.html`: operational versioned public terms and engineering disclaimer.
- `hub.html`: recurring-use utility with role filters, twelve visible-formula calculators, explainers, source search, and a clearly labelled AI beta.

## Claim taxonomy

Every public claim must be one of:

1. **Visible now** — the visitor can inspect the method, output structure, calculator, policy, or control.
2. **Verified per engagement** — named competency, registration, standards experience, reference, conflict, or insurance evidence supplied for the actual scope.
3. **Constructed demonstration** — a fictional but realistic scenario, explicitly labelled and never presented as client work.
4. **Future intent** — ambition or roadmap language that does not imply existing authority.
5. **Not claimed** — government mandate, undisclosed client history, certification, partnership, completed project, or result without authorized evidence.

## Demonstration-case rule

A demonstration must include:

- the predominant question;
- evidence requested;
- alternative routes;
- intended decision package;
- explicit “not a client project” disclosure;
- no fabricated organization, contract value, location, date, endorsement, or measured outcome.

## Owner verification needed before production

Publish only verified and approved facts:

- legal entity name and status;
- registration and tax identifiers where appropriate;
- accountable business contact and official address;
- named professional roster and registrations;
- insurance coverage;
- authorized project references or an explicit “references available under NDA” process;
- sector-specific standards and classification experience;
- anti-bribery, conflict, sanctions/export-control, HSE, QA and document-control statements;
- privacy/terms legal review for the governing jurisdiction.

Absence is preferable to invented confidence.
