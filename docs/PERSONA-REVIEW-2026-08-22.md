Date: 2026-08-22

This is a structured simulation of likely visitor reactions, not an endorsement or statement made by any named person or institution. Every perspective was compared on the same six points: **capture, relevance, credibility, utility, risk comfort, and likely action**.

## Consensus

1. The design is no longer the primary weakness.
2. The problem-first philosophy is distinctive and memorable.
3. Junior and field audiences need deeper practical, recurring utility.
4. Senior technical audiences need evidence, accountability and sector competence.
5. Institutional visitors need security, governance, legal identity and procurement readiness.
6. The AI assistant must remain a limited beta, never an engineering decision-maker.

## Honest launch verdict

> Love at first sight: achieved. Trust sufficient for procurement: not achieved.

The central risk is an exceptionally written vision appearing ahead of the institution that must substantiate it. The correct response is not to reduce the vision or fabricate history. It is to add inspectable method evidence, authorized capability facts, samples, governance, sector boundaries and a clear engagement route.

## Priority order retained from the review

1. Institutional identity and verifiable legal/contact facts
2. Capability evidence and labeled constructed demonstrations
3. Clear engagement stages, deliverables and responsibility boundaries
4. Trust centre: providers, privacy, security, retention and procurement readiness
5. Role-based repeat-use Engineering Hub
6. Sector-specific language and evidence as it becomes authorized
7. AI beta positioning, privacy confirmation, refusal behavior and graceful failure
