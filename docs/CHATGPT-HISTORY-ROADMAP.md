### AP v3.0.1 — Hub correction and owner rejection

The owner rejected an over-expanded Hub redesign that substituted synthetic topic combinations for real engineering knowledge, introduced a broken `ask.html` route and removed the original AI experience.

Durable lesson:

- Do not equate quantity with knowledge.
- Do not call generated combinations “500 articles” or “500 routes.”
- Preserve the successful university-map layout and visual transitions.
- Make department cards functional.
- Keep the scientific calculator directly useful.
- Move the 50 engineering calculators to a dedicated library when implemented properly.
- Delete shallow four-card explainers unless replaced by genuine searchable knowledge.
- Treat AI as a broad, source-aware engineering companion; four hard-coded fallback topics are not sufficient.
- Do not expose implementation commentary to ordinary visitors.

The rejected layout was rolled back. The original Hub structure and theory companion were restored; calculator discovery was narrowed without changing the broad page rhythm.
