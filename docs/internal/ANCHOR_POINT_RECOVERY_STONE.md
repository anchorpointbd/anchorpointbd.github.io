# Anchor Point recovery stone

Anchor Point is a problem-first engineering institution built around **Engineering at Ease**, the institutional standard **Anchored in Excellence**, and the public proposition **See the real problem. Choose the next move.** Its method is **Understand → Diagnose → Explore → Decide → Deliver**: make the question, evidence, assumptions, alternatives, uncertainty, consequences, and next action visible.

The architecture is institutional, not founder-centric. Do not invent maturity, multidisciplinary staffing, clients, projects, certifications, government authority, results, offices, or international scale. Founder experience may inform the origin and constructed demonstrations, but private personal material stays private and claims remain evidence-led.

The website direction is a connected journey: public homepage and clear engagement routes; About/origin; Method/How We Work; Services/engagement; an Engineering Ecosystem with faculties, departments, pathways, calculators, conversion/scientific desks, and authoritative external knowledge; and a separately governed private Vault. Third-party knowledge remains with its publisher.
