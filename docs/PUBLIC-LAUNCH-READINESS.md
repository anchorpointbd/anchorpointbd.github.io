# Public launch readiness

## Implemented in the proof-layer candidate

- Inspectable capability/evidence page with no fabricated history.
- Clearly labelled marine, industrial, public-project, procurement, and international-coordination fields.
- Three constructed method demonstrations.
- Five sample output structures and a predominant-question brief preview.
- Four-stage first-engagement path.
- Public trust centre with information classification and channel rules.
- Current provider/data map for Cloudflare, Supabase, OpenRouter, Zoho, and GitHub.
- Explicit AI beta, consent gate, confidentiality warning, safe unavailable state, and non-AI fallback utility.
- Twelve calculators with formulas and assumptions.
- Role filters for everyday, field, student, project, commercial, and finance visitors.
- Four engineering explainers and searchable open resources.
- Versioned privacy notice and public terms.
- Sitemap entries and noindex protection for Vault/invitation paths.

## Owner facts still required

These cannot be inferred from code or branding:

- legal entity name, form, registration number, and authorized publication wording;
- verified office/address wording;
- accountable public contact role;
- professional registrations, qualifications, competency matrix, and publication permission;
- insurance status;
- tax/VAT details appropriate for public or proposal-only disclosure;
- real project references and client permission;
- HSE, QA/QC, anti-bribery, conflict, sanctions/export-control and document-control approvals;
- governing law and jurisdiction for final legal terms;
- privacy/data retention periods and named backup custodian;
- whether WhatsApp remains a public contact channel;
- whether Bangladesh/bilingual content is required at launch.

## Production gate

Do not merge or deploy to the production domain until the owner:

1. reviews the preview on desktop and mobile;
2. supplies or deliberately withholds each verification item above;
3. approves the privacy notice and terms for legal review;
4. completes live Vault invitation/TOTP tests for both staff;
5. completes a public AI request test and confirms OpenRouter activity;
6. verifies forms, email, DNS, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, domain redirect and HTTPS;
7. authorizes removal of preview-wide `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`;
8. explicitly approves production deployment.
