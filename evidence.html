When We Can Help

Bring us in at any useful stage.

Anchor Point can help when work is new, moving, operating or already in trouble.

  1. EARLY IDEAExample: test whether the project need is clear before choosing equipment or a delivery route.
  2. PLANNINGExample: check assumptions, interfaces and missing evidence before the plan hardens.
  3. PROCUREMENTExample: compare bids, scope gaps, vendor deviations and their consequences.
  4. DELIVERYExample: identify which interface or change needs a decision before work is delayed.
  5. OPERATIONExample: examine why actual output, reliability or cost differs from expectation.
  6. RECOVERYExample: find the controlling problem and compare realistic recovery sequences.

How We Work With You

Keep the decision visible.

Each engagement keeps the question, evidence, choices and final decision clear.

Question→Evidence→Constraints→Options→Comparison→Decision→Record
ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

Why is the pump not reaching expected output?

Evidence: operating data, drawings, pump curve, system resistance and site conditions. Constraints: existing piping, power, budget and downtime.

Options: adjust operation, modify the system, change equipment or investigate another cause. We compare consequence, cost, risk and confidence before choosing the next action and recording why.

Who Does What

Clear responsibility protects the work.

We keep advice, specialist authority and the client's final decision separate.

ANCHOR POINT-LED

Question framing, evidence organization, independent review, option comparison, coordination and decision records within an agreed scope.

SPECIALIST-REQUIRED

Work that needs discipline-specific verification, licensed practice, statutory review, testing, certification or other properly appointed expertise.

CLIENT-RETAINED

Commercial commitment, formal instruction, risk acceptance, regulatory submission and final organizational authority unless lawfully delegated.
