How We Work With You

One question, carried through seven visible steps.

This pump-underperformance story is illustrative. It is not a client project, verified diagnosis or design recommendation.

  1. 01 · QUESTION

    Why is the pump not reaching expected output?

    We agree what “expected” means, which operating case matters and who needs the answer.

  2. 02 · EVIDENCE

    Build a usable picture.

    Operating flow and pressure, pump curve, system layout, power and load data, maintenance condition and site observations.

  3. 03 · CONSTRAINTS

    Make the real limits visible.

    Existing piping, available power, downtime, budget, required duty and procurement lead time.

  4. 04 · OPTIONS

    Keep credible paths open.

    Operational correction, maintenance or repair, system modification, equipment change, or further testing and instrumentation.

  5. 05 · COMPARISON

    Compare more than purchase price.

    For each credible option: expected effect, cost, downtime, risk, reversibility, evidence confidence and consequences.

  6. 06 · DECISION

    Choose the supported next step.

    State the recommended action together with remaining uncertainty, conditions and any check required before commitment.

  7. 07 · RECORD

    Keep the reason.

    A short decision note preserves the evidence, assumptions, rejected and retained options, reason for the choice and review trigger.

Who Does What

Clear responsibility protects the work.

We keep advice, specialist authority and the client's final decision separate.

ANCHOR POINT-LED

Question framing, evidence organization, independent review, option comparison, coordination and decision records within an agreed scope.

SPECIALIST-REQUIRED

Work that needs discipline-specific verification, licensed practice, statutory review, testing, certification or other properly appointed expertise.

CLIENT-RETAINED

Commercial commitment, formal instruction, risk acceptance, regulatory submission and final organizational authority unless lawfully delegated.
