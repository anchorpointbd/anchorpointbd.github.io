Why Anchor Point began

From a napkin sketch
to a future worth building.

It began with a frustration many capable people know: having more to contribute than the system has room to receive.

Across Bangladesh, young engineers graduate wanting to solve problems, improve organizations and build work that matters. Then opportunity narrows, survival becomes urgent, and capability is too often reduced to compliance.

Capability does not disappear when it is overlooked. It becomes disconnected.

The gap we felt · the question that remained

Useful capability can be present—and still have nowhere to connect.

Anchor Point began by reframing that gap in plain terms: not as a shortage of capable people, but as a need for a clearer place where different disciplines can meet around a real problem.
