Method · How will they handle my problem?

The reasoning should
remain visible.

Decisions are easier to trust when another person can see what was known, what was assumed, which alternatives were considered and why the next move was chosen.

A clear answer matters. An inspectable path to it matters too.

Working promise

Not artificially simple answers.
Inspectable reasoning.

We make the question, evidence, assumptions, constraints, options and consequences visible at a depth appropriate to the decision.

If the reasoning cannot be examined, it cannot be responsibly challenged, continued or improved.

Core method

Five movements through a difficult decision.

The sequence stays consistent. The depth changes with the question.

01

Understand

What must become possible, for whom, and what is already known?

Agree what success means before discussing solutions.
02

Diagnose

What is the real cause, and what is only a symptom?

Separate the visible concern from the system condition creating it.
03

Explore

Which credible options exist, and what travels with each?

Keep trade-offs, uncertainty and escape routes visible.
04

Decide

Which option does the available evidence support?

Compare consequences before commitment.
05

Deliver

What useful action, record or next step should remain?

Leave responsibility and reasoning ready to continue.

Example decisions

Illustrations of the reasoning—not client case studies.

Details are deliberately generic. They demonstrate how the question changes when the system is examined.

01 · Technical review“Should we accept the larger pump?”

Reframe: What must the system move, against what pressure, under which operating cases—and which option meets that need without unnecessary energy, wear or risk?

Illustrative only. Not a design recommendation or verified project credential.
02 · Procurement deviation“Is this supplier deviation acceptable?”

Reframe: Which requirement changed, what technical and commercial consequences follow, what evidence closes the gap, and who retains approval authority?

Illustrative only. Actual evaluation requires the governing documents and appointed responsibilities.
03 · Project delay“How do we recover the schedule?”

Reframe: Which constraint is controlling the delay, which dependencies move with it, what recovery paths are credible, and what new risk each path creates?

Illustrative only. Not a claim of completed recovery work.

Responsibility & boundaries

Visible reasoning does not blur accountability.

Retain commercial, organizational and formal decision authority unless lawfully delegated.
