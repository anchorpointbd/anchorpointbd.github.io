01 · Learning

Short enough to use.
Serious enough to question.

Each note begins with a practical misconception and ends with the questions that must remain open.

ELECTRICAL · 4 MIN

Power factor is not “lost power.”

Improving power factor can reduce current for the same real power, but correction still depends on harmonics, switching and operating variation.

Questions before action
  • Was the measurement taken at the relevant operating state?
  • Are harmonics present?
  • Who verifies the correction equipment?
SYSTEMS · 5 MIN

A pump does not choose its operating point alone.

The pump curve and system curve meet at the operating point. Static head, friction, controls, fluid properties and speed move that intersection.

Questions before action
  • Is required flow measured or assumed?
  • What creates total dynamic head?
  • Has cavitation margin been verified?
MATERIALS · 5 MIN

Heat input is a clue, not a complete procedure.

Voltage, current and travel speed affect estimated heat input. Joint geometry, thickness, preheat and technique still shape the thermal cycle.

Questions before action
  • Which procedure and variables apply?
  • How was travel speed measured?
  • Which toughness constraints govern?
DECISIONS · 6 MIN

Lowest price and lowest lifecycle cost are different questions.

Availability, energy, spares, maintenance skill, downtime, interfaces, warranty and escape cost can change the consequence of ownership.

Questions before action
  • Which costs differ between options?
  • Whose budget carries downtime?
  • Which assumption could reverse the ranking?
