Contact enquiry privacy notice

When you send an enquiry, we use the information you provide only to understand and respond to your request.

What we receive

Your name, email address, selected project category, and message are sent through our contact-form provider, Formspree, to the Anchor Point enquiry inbox.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to you, assess whether we can help, and keep a record of our communication where necessary. We do not sell enquiry information.

Third-party services

This website uses Formspree to process the contact form. Links to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and other third-party services are governed by their own privacy practices.
