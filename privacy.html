Privacy

Anchor Point’s public pages do not intentionally use advertising cookies. Server and hosting providers may retain security and access logs. Email enquiries are used to respond, scope work, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations.

Private Vault

Vault identity, project records, and documents are accessible only to authorized staff. Access events and changes may be logged. Do not upload unnecessary personal data, credentials, export-controlled material, or safety-critical records until an approved classification and retention schedule applies.

Requests

For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact info@anchorpoint.com.bd. Some records may be retained where required for contracts, security, or law.
